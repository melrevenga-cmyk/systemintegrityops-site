A 90-day outbound pilot to open US pipeline for AST

Precision outreach and warm introductions — built to land qualified conversations with US luxury and design-led brands that fit AST's bespoke digital work.

This is quality over volume. AST sells discretion, judgement, and craft to high-expectation brands, so the outreach is written and paced to match — small, hand-picked target lists and personalized contact, never mass cold spam that would sit at odds with the brand. You keep the client relationship and the delivery; I build and run the engine that fills the top of your funnel.

01Scope of work

A · LinkedIn — presence and outreach

  • Position AST for a US luxury audience — company page language and framing that reflects the studio's calm, considered brand voice.
  • Targeted connection requests and personalized outreach to decision-makers at US luxury and design-led brands.
  • Warm engagement — connect, follow, thoughtfully comment on prospect activity before any direct outreach — so the AST name is familiar and credible when contact arrives.
  • A light, considered content cadence anchored on AST's strengths — bespoke digital experiences, creative design, and AI visibility — to build authority with a discerning audience.

B · Email outbound — precision, not volume

  • Define the US target profile with you across AST's verticals — luxury fashion & lifestyle, hospitality & travel, health & wellness, professional & advisory, creative & entertainment — and build a hand-researched shortlist of brands that genuinely fit AST's best work.
  • Deep research and verified contact data for each target — personalization per brand, not a templated blast.
  • Outbound sent from properly configured, warmed sending infrastructure — deliverability handled so emails reach the inbox. This is the part I do every day.
  • Messaging built on AST's real differentiators: bespoke digital craft and AI visibility (being found when buyers ask AI assistants for recommendations in their category) — a current, relevant hook few competitors are using.
  • Reply triage, meeting booking, and follow-up throughout — qualified conversations handed to you to close.

C · Partner & referral outreach

  • Luxury moves on trust and introductions — so alongside direct outreach, I'll build relationships with firms adjacent to AST's world: luxury PR agencies, brand and identity studios, high-end architects and interior designers, and luxury marketing consultants.
  • These partners serve the same brands but don't build bespoke digital experiences — making AST a natural referral for work they can't deliver themselves.
  • Warm partner outreach run on the same infrastructure — one good introduction into a luxury network can outperform months of cold contact.

02Timeline — 90-day pilot

Days 1–14

Setup & research. Target profile and hand-researched shortlist defined with you across AST's verticals. Sending infrastructure configured and warmed. Personalized sequences drafted and tested. LinkedIn positioning updated; warming and partner mapping begin.

Days 15–60

Outreach. Direct and partner outreach launch to the shortlist. LinkedIn engagement and content active throughout. Replies triaged, meetings booked and confirmed in real time. Early learnings sharpen the messaging and targeting.

Days 61–90

Iterate & document. Strong-signal segments and partner relationships get a second push. The full system — target research, sequences, infrastructure, partner list, and playbook — is documented for AST to keep running. Day 90 review on whether the pilot continues.

03What success looks like

Defined together
For luxury work, a handful of genuinely well-matched conversations matters far more than a high count — each closed engagement is significant. Rather than fix a number here, we'll set the right targets and the metrics that matter to AST together on our first call, so they fit your goals, capacity, and how you define a good outcome.

Likely worth tracking: reply quality · connection accept rate · meetings booked · partner relationships opened · pipeline value created — we'll agree the shortlist that matters to you.

One closed US engagement at AST's typical value pays back the pilot many times over. We'll hold short reviews at Day 30, Day 60, and Day 90: Day 30 is the first read on outreach performance, Day 60 is the decision point on where to double down, and Day 90 is where we decide whether the engagement continues.
