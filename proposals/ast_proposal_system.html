A proposal system AST owns and runs

Turning your most manual, delay-prone task — building a bespoke proposal — into a repeatable flow that keeps your pricing judgment in your hands and gives you a live view of your pipeline.

The problem

You told me the most tedious, delay-prone part of your work is creating a bespoke proposal. Right now every proposal is rebuilt from scratch: scope is pulled from memory after a call, pricing is re-estimated by hand each time with no written rate sheet, and the document is assembled page by page. There's also no single place to see where each prospect sits between enquiry and a signed contract.

What I'll build

A connected proposal system you own and run without me. It uses the LLM and call tools you already work with — no new platforms to learn — and everything lives in one Google workspace.

Step 1Intake from the call

Your call transcript feeds a tested prompt that pulls the scope, timeline, and details into a structured intake — so a proposal starts from complete, consistent inputs without you filling anything in by hand. A quick glance confirms it before it drives pricing.

Step 2Rate sheet

A one-time build of your line items and baseline rates, so scope prices itself — with a strategic-adjustment field that keeps full control of the final number in your hands. Yours to update whenever your pricing changes.

Step 3Proposal draft

A tested prompt for the LLM you already use turns the intake and pricing into a first-draft proposal in your voice and template. You edit and add the strategic framing.

Step 4Pipeline tracker

A simple Google Sheet where every prospect is logged as it moves — so nothing stalls unseen, and you can see your close rate and average deal value at a glance.

The four pieces, connected — and where every prospect is tracked.

The outcome

  • Proposal turnaround drops from hours to minutes.
  • Every proposal is consistent, complete, and in your voice.
  • Pricing stops leaking margin — the rate sheet does the arithmetic, you make the call.
  • Pipeline visibility you don't have today, including the customer and deal-size data that helps price everything else.

Delivered as one defined build and handed over: the intake prompt, the rate sheet, the draft workflow with a short how-to, and the tracker. Yours to run — no dependency on me afterward.
