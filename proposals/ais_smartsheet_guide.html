Section 2

About Smartsheet

A cloud work management platform that behaves like Excel, but is built for collaboration and visibility.

Smartsheet helps teams plan, track, automate, and report on work. It performs similarly to Excel but with enhanced functionality, and it is optimized for collaboration, visibility, and productivity. AIS has pre-configured the application for your integration activities, so it is relatively easy to use — though there is a small learning curve.

Three types of documents

Sheets

The foundation of the platform and the single source of truth for all underlying data.

Reports

Consolidate and filter data from multiple sheets into one view and location.

Dashboards

Consolidate sheet and report data into a visual summary of where things stand.

You enter data in a spreadsheet or project plan, track progress through automated reports, and present that data visually on dashboards. Alerts and automated workflows let you build efficiency into your sheets without any coding.

Why the platform is trusted

Smartsheet is used by over 90% of the Fortune 100 and 80% of the Fortune 500. It began as a project management tool and has since expanded into an extremely flexible platform with solutions for operations, IT, marketing, content management, healthcare, human resources, finance, sales, supply chain, and more.

  • Secure — governed through strict administrative controls.
  • Scalable — from small teams to large enterprises, Smartsheet can scale its solutions as you grow.
  • Unified — connects to everyday productivity apps including Microsoft Teams, Slack, Tableau, Salesforce, Adobe Creative Cloud, and Google Drive. See the full list of partner solutions.
  • User driven — every licensed user can manage projects, automate workflows, and build solutions without advanced coding skills.

To learn more, visit the Why Smartsheet page.

Section 3

Licenses and logging in

What kind of account you have, and how to get into it.

License types

Smartsheet requires a plan-based account before anyone can get started. Your Smartsheet System Administrator sets up the initial account and adds users. On Business and Enterprise accounts there are two license types:

LicenseWhat it isWhat it can do
Licensed userIssued a paid license by the system administratorCan own and administer items within the platform
Free userA collaborator added to specific itemsCan edit, comment on, or view items

The experience for both license types is nearly identical, from initial login to navigating the platform. The only difference is access to certain features that depend on your permission level. For details, see Plan-Level User Types.

How this works on AIS projectsLicenses are limited to designated Consultants and/or Clients. Integration Team members (Clients and/or Guests) are granted Editor, Commenter, or Viewer permissions. Unless specifically discussed and agreed with the Client, the Administrator will be AIS.

Logging in

Go to app.smartsheet.com. If you have been given a license by the system administrator — or already have one through your company — sign in with your username and password. You can also use single sign-on through Google, Microsoft, or Apple, depending on your email domain.

If you are a free user logging in for the first time, you can create an account there and use a 30-day free trial. There is no obligation to switch to a paid account to keep collaborating after the trial ends.

Section 4

Navigating the platform

The left ribbon is your map. Here's what each icon does.

When you first log in you land on the Home page, where relevant items are suggested based on your use. The left ribbon holds everything else.

The folder icon is your main routeClicking it opens a navigation pane on the left and a much larger viewing pane on the right. In the navigation pane you'll see a Sheets folder and a people icon for Workspaces. Workspaces are collaborative spaces for related files; the Sheets folder is the catch-all for anything created by you or shared with you individually. The viewing pane shows a folder's contents plus sharing status, owner, and last update.

Section 5

Working inside a sheet

Text formatting, images, and formulas work much like Excel. These four toolbar features don't.

View changer Filter Conditional formatting Cell linking

View changer

Switch between Grid view for spreadsheets, Gantt view for project management, Card view for agile task management, Timeline view for presentations, and Calendar view to place tasks on a calendar. AIS typically defaults to Grid/Gantt, but whichever way you enter data, all views populate automatically.

Filter

Build complex filters within a sheet — set multiple conditions, save filters for later, and share them with anyone else who has access to the sheet.

Conditional formatting

Create rules per column that apply to whole rows or specific cells when conditions are met: custom shading, text color, font size, strikethrough, and more. Rules are stacked, so the ones at the top take priority over those below. Some conditional formatting rules are already set in the AIS templates.

Cell linking

Connect data from one cell to another in an external sheet. The data flow is one-way and cannot be overwritten without disconnecting the link. Smartsheet marks a blue triangle on the right side of a cell for incoming data and a gray triangle for outgoing data.

Collaboration icons on the right

  • Conversation / chat — sends your message to the contacts you designate and attaches it to the task your cursor is on.
  • Attachment — add files directly to a task in a worksheet.
  • Proofs — leave annotated comments on files and worksheets.
  • Update request — get status updates from team members.
  • Publish — turn worksheets into a PDF you can share.

Automations

Automations let you receive alerts or update requests, create approval workflows, change row data, and move or copy rows to another sheet. Each one starts from a trigger — a sheet action, or a date being reached — plus any conditions you set. Open them with Automation → Manage Workflows on the top menu bar.

Already done for youSome automations are already implemented on AIS templates. Check what exists before building your own. To learn more, see automation in Smartsheet University.

Section 6

Sharing and permissions

Two levels of sharing, and six levels of access. Pick a role below to see what it can do.

What you can share

You can share an entire workspace or folder when collaborators need to see all the files in one place. Consultants and client team members both use this — but be cautious about sensitive or proprietary data held inside.

Alternatively, share individual items such as a sheet, report, or dashboard for a given workstream. Use this for clients or internal users who only need a few items. A shared individual item shows up in the recipient's general Sheets folder, and they can move it into a folder in their own workspace.

Checking who has accessWhen viewing a workspace, the right panel labeled "Workspace Shared To" shows who can see everything. For individually shared items, a blue person icon appears to the right of the Sharing column — hover over it to see exactly who has access to that item.

The AIS Engagement Leader(s) act as Administrator and work with Consultants and Clients to determine participants and their access levels.

Section 7

Your profile, notifications, and help

Ten minutes of setup that changes how everyone else sees you — and how much email you get.

Set up your profile

  1. Open Personal Settings

    Click the Account icon in the bottom left corner and select Personal Settings.

  2. Fill in your details

    Upload a profile photo and enter your name, title, department, company, and contact information. These details are visible to everyone else in the platform when tasks are assigned to you.

  3. Check your settings

    On the left of the dialog box, under Profile, you'll find settings, notifications, mobile devices, apps, and API access. Under Settings, set how often Smartsheet autosaves and choose your time zone — this controls how every timestamp is displayed to you.

Notifications

Smartsheet notifies you about sheet changes and reminders, @mentions, sharing invitations, proof requests, update and approval requests, and conversation replies. By default these are pushed through email, the mobile app, and messaging apps such as Teams and Slack where applicable.

To change them, click the Account icon in the bottom left corner, select Personal Settings, then Notifications, and uncheck anything you don't want to receive.

Notifications also appear under the bell icon on the left bar, where you can see all notifications and requests for you, clear them all at the bottom, delete specific ones, or unsubscribe. More detail is in the Smartsheet articles on the Notification Center and configuring how you're notified.

Mobile app

The mobile app lets you plan, execute, and track work on the go — editing data, submitting forms, adding attachments, replying to comments, and receiving notifications. It's available for Android and Apple devices, and there's more information here.

Getting help

The question mark (?) icon on the bottom left ribbon links to the Smartsheet Community, where you can search for answers or post questions for experts, and to Smartsheet University for help articles on any topic. Section 11 has a fuller list.

Help is on almost every pageOn the Browse page, a question mark in the top right corner pops up tips for that section. Inside a sheet, many column headers carry informational links, and when you write a formula a popup walks you through the syntax and links to more information.

Section 8

Project workspaces

What you'll find in your workstream folder, and what each file is for.

Access to project files and workspaces varies by role. Clients receive access to shared AIS project templates optimized for consultants and clients alike: the workstream contact list, playbook, and RAID log all live in the Sheets folder. Clients are also given links to the project dashboard and Lessons Learned form by their Consultant or the Smartsheet Administrator.

Consultants issued a license by the Smartsheet Administrator get a shared AIS project workspace with a more comprehensive template set for task tracking and risk management.

The Workstream folder

Individual workstream templates live here. Selecting one — Finance-Accounting, for example — shows the following:

ItemWhat it's for
Project Playbook (sheet)Track the status of every task required to complete the project: health, priority, % complete, start date, finish date, duration, dependencies, owner/lead, last update, and baseline/variance.
Project Playbook DashboardVisualize overall project health and status through configured charts, metrics, reports, and hyperlinks.
Project RAID Log (sheet)Track risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies, questions, and changes in one place.
Key Contacts (sheet)Track every project stakeholder: name, title, project role, knowledge area, and which communications they need to be included in.
Lessons Learned Log (sheet)Identify and track lessons learned as you go. Use the included intake form to collect feedback from stakeholders.

The Reports & Metrics folder

Inside each workstream folder sits a Reports & Metrics folder. For Finance-Accounting it looks like this:

What every report in this folder shows
  • At-Risk Tasks Report — tasks with either a "Red Circle" health or "Stuck" status.
  • Current User Report — tasks assigned to you where % complete is under 100% and the finish date falls within the next 30 days.
  • Key Contacts (report) — project stakeholders and their roles, displayed on the project dashboard.
  • Key Tasks Report — all tasks in the first two levels of the project plan's hierarchy, for display on the dashboard.
  • Open RAID Items (report) — RAID log items with any status other than Closed: Open, On Hold, Not Started, or In Progress.
  • Overall Progress (report) — lets the Workstream Update be edited and listed on the Portfolio Status Tracking sheet and shown on the dashboard. Workstream updates can also be edited directly on the Portfolio Status Tracking sheet.
  • Overdue / Upcoming Activities Report — tasks under 100% complete whose start or finish date falls within the next 30 days.
  • Project Metrics (sheet) — overall status, % complete, variance, tasks by status, and tasks by health, for display on the dashboard. Pre-configured and modified only by the Administrator(s).
  • Recent Accomplishments Report — tasks that hit 100% complete with a finish date in the last 30 days.
  • Tasks by Priority (report) — groups tasks from Critical to Low.
  • Tasks by Status (report) — groups tasks as Stuck, Not Started, In Progress, Pending, and Complete.

Section 9

Finalizing project setup

What the Administrator or Lead Consultant completes before a workstream goes live.

The Smartsheet Administrator handles initial setup of the project templates used by Workstream Leads. After that, a few finalization steps are completed by the Administrator (or the Lead Consultant on a workstream) to make the setup complete. These cover the Key Contacts, Playbook, RAID Log, Lessons Learned Log, and Project Metrics sheets, plus one final task in the Overall Progress report.

Playbook

Open the playbook and modify the included tasks and other cells as applicable. Assign the correct health, priority, status, % complete, start, finish, and owner/lead to each task, and adjust dependencies.

Three things to know about the playbook columns
  • WBS is assigned automatically based on hierarchy level.
  • Duration is calculated from the number of business days between start and finish.
  • Predecessors define the relationship between tasks and reference the row number of related tasks.

Connect as many tasks as you can. If delays hit, linked start dates shift automatically; without those connections you have to change every downstream start date by hand. See enabling dependencies using predecessors for more.

Key Contacts

  1. Replace the placeholder stakeholders

    Open the Key Contacts sheet and replace the names, titles, project roles, and knowledge areas with your own stakeholders.

  2. Set the communication checkboxes

    Customize the checkbox columns to indicate which communication types each stakeholder should receive, based on their role in the project.

RAID Log

Open the RAID Log and replace the test rows with any risks, issues, assumptions, dependencies, questions, or changes that apply to your project.

Last step — Administrator onlyThe Smartsheet Administrator is responsible for setting up both the Playbook Baseline and Project Metrics. This assures consistency across the workstream portfolio.

Setting baselines

Once there is confidence in the planned start and finish dates, the Smartsheet Administrator baselines your schedule — typically 2–4 weeks after the initial playbooks are populated.

Baselining copies your current Start and Finish columns into a new set of columns called Baseline Start and Baseline Finish. It also adds a Variance column that tracks the duration difference between your current duration and the baseline duration, so you can see how far ahead or behind plan you are.

Section 10

Using the dashboard

The fastest way to see — and show — where the project stands.

The project dashboard visualizes overall progress through metrics, graphs, and reports that tell the story of where the project stands at any point in time.

  • Workstream Objectives is a Rich Text widget. Use it to outline freely what you're looking to accomplish in the project.
  • Refresh rate defaults to every 10 minutes. Change it with the View button on the top menu.
  • Presentation Mode sits in the top right corner. Use it during meetings as a full-screen view so your audience focuses on the content.
  • Snapshots — to capture the dashboard at a point in time, print to PDF.
Stop rebuilding status slidesThe main point of dashboards is to present from them directly rather than copying data into a slide deck. That's the time savings.
